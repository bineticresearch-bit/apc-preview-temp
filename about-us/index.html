Who we are

A Vancouver company, not a call center

All Pro Duct Cleaning LLC is a locally owned company based in Vancouver, Washington. Our work is focused on the parts of a home that are easy to ignore: the duct system behind the registers, the dryer vent inside the wall, and the crawl space under the floor.

We are licensed in Washington. We do not publish a license number on this site because the original business listing does not include one; if you need verification for a property manager or insurer, call us and we will provide the information we have.

Our approach is simple. Inspect the system, explain what we find, quote the work before it begins, and show the customer what came out when it is finished. No franchise script and no pressure to add services that the home does not need.

  • Whole-home air duct cleaning with negative-air HEPA equipment
  • Dryer vent cleaning included with every full duct cleaning
  • Crawl space cleanup, insulation, mold remediation, and vapor barriers
  • Accessible duct repair, sealing, and insulation

What we do

Four parts of a healthier home

Duct cleaning is the core of our business. Crawl space work is the other half of the moisture and air quality picture.

Air ducts

Supply and return cleaning, accessible furnace components, and a visual presentation of the debris removed.

Dryer vents

The full vent run from the dryer connection to the exterior hood, included free with a full duct cleaning.

Crawl spaces

Cleanup, exclusion, insulation, mold remediation, and vapor barrier repair or replacement.

Duct repair

Accessible flex and rigid duct repair, joint sealing, and insulation for exposed runs.

Every serviceThe same seven-step process, start to finish.

How we work

The details are the service

We show you the clean equipment before it goes into the home. We isolate the furnace, seal the registers, and keep the system under negative pressure while each run is cleaned. We put furniture back, clean up the work area, and show you the vacuum stages before we hand you the invoice.

That is not a marketing promise. It is the process we follow on every full duct cleaning, whether the home has eight vents or twenty-eight.

A clear scope

What we do — and what we leave to other trades

A good contractor should be honest about the limits of the work.

What we handle

  • Residential air duct cleaning and dryer vent cleaning
  • Accessible HVAC duct repair, sealing, and insulation
  • Furnace filter replacement when the customer supplies the filter
  • Crawl space cleanup, exclusion, insulation, mold remediation, and vapor barriers

What we do not do

  • Furnace repair or routine furnace maintenance
  • Live animal trapping or pest control
  • Structural foundation repair or engineering
  • Work inside a finished wall without a proper access opening

If a problem needs a different trade, we will tell you instead of covering it up.
