Lint, airflow, and fire safety

Dryer Vent Cleaning in Vancouver, WA & Portland, OR

Lint buildup is a leading contributor to dryer fires. We clean the full vent run, not just the lint trap.

A dryer vent is one of the most overlooked safety systems in a home. Every load of laundry sends lint into the vent line. Some of it sticks to the duct walls, and over time that buildup restricts airflow, makes clothes take longer to dry, and can create a fire hazard.

All Pro cleans the entire vent run from the dryer connection to the exterior hood. We also check the transition duct, lint trap area, and exterior flap so you can see whether the system is moving air the way it should.

What is included

  • Disconnecting the dryer and inspecting the transition duct.
  • Cleaning the full vent run with specialized dryer vent tools.
  • Removing lint from the lint trap housing and exterior hood.
  • Checking the exterior damper for proper operation.
  • Reconnecting the dryer and confirming airflow.
  • A free dryer vent cleaning with every full air duct cleaning.

Signs you may need this service

  • Clothes take more than one cycle to dry.
  • The dryer feels hot to the touch or the top gets unusually warm.
  • You notice a burning or scorched smell during a cycle.
  • Lint accumulates around the exterior vent hood.
  • The vent run is long, has multiple elbows, or is hidden inside a wall.

How the service works

Inspect the run

We locate the dryer, transition duct, and exterior termination, then check for crush points or disconnected sections.

Clean the full line

The vent is cleaned from the dryer end to the exterior hood. We remove lint from the housing and confirm the damper moves freely.

Reconnect and test

The dryer is reconnected, pushed back carefully, and run briefly so we can confirm airflow and check for leaks.

Frequently asked questions

How often should a dryer vent be cleaned?

Once a year is a practical baseline for most households. Homes with heavy laundry use, long vent runs, or pets may need more frequent service.

Is dryer vent cleaning included with duct cleaning?

Yes. A dryer vent cleaning is included free with every full air duct cleaning.

Can you clean a roof vent or a long concealed run?

In most cases, yes. We inspect the route first and use tools sized for the run. If a section is inaccessible or damaged, we explain the options before work begins.

My dryer still is not drying well after cleaning. What next?

Airflow can also be affected by a clogged lint screen, a failing dryer, a crushed transition duct, or a damper that will not open. We check the vent components we can access and tell you what we find.
