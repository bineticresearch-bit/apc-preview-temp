Good questions

Common questions about duct and vent service

What is included in a full air duct cleaning?

A full cleaning includes the supply and return ducts, accessible furnace components such as the blower area and A/C coils, the plenums, and a free dryer vent cleaning. We clean vent by vent with the system under negative air pressure and show you the debris when we are finished.

Do you clean dryer vents too?

Yes. Dryer vent cleaning is included free with every full air duct cleaning. We clean the entire vent run from the dryer connection to the exterior hood and check the damper.

How long does the service take?

Most whole-home cleanings take three to four hours. The time depends on the number of vents, the layout of the system, and how much buildup is present.

Do you repair furnaces?

No. We clean accessible furnace components as part of duct cleaning, but we do not repair furnaces or perform routine furnace maintenance. A qualified HVAC technician should handle equipment repair.
