A whole-home approach

Indoor Air Quality Services in Vancouver, WA & Portland, OR

Duct cleaning is one piece of the puzzle. We look at filtration, moisture, and the crawl space too.

Indoor air quality is affected by more than the inside of a duct. Dust, pollen, pet dander, humidity, crawl space moisture, filtration, and ventilation all play a role. Cleaning the ductwork can remove a reservoir of debris, but it will not correct a wet crawl space or a filter that is never changed.

We take a practical, whole-home view. During the visit we look at the duct system, accessible furnace components, filter condition, return air paths, and any crawl space moisture we can see. Then we explain what is likely contributing to the problem and what can realistically be improved.

What is included

  • A walkthrough focused on dust, odors, airflow, and moisture.
  • Whole-home air duct cleaning when the system shows buildup.
  • Filter condition and filter-size guidance.
  • Crawl space inspection for moisture, damaged insulation, or a failing vapor barrier.
  • Dryer vent cleaning and airflow checks.
  • Straightforward recommendations for source control, filtration, and humidity.

Signs you may need this service

  • Dust returns quickly after cleaning.
  • Family members notice allergies or irritation when the system runs.
  • The home has a persistent musty smell.
  • Windows sweat, or the crawl space feels damp.
  • You have pets, recent remodeling, or a poorly maintained filter.

How the service works

Listen and inspect

We ask about symptoms, pets, remodels, and moisture, then inspect the accessible system and crawl space.

Clean what needs cleaning

If the ducts, dryer vent, or crawl space have buildup, we recommend the service that addresses the source rather than a collection of add-ons.

Address the cause

We explain filtration, humidity, ventilation, and source-control steps that may be needed after cleaning.

Have a question about your home? Call 360-903-9030. We will ask about the system and tell you whether this service is the right fit.

Frequently asked questions

Do you perform air quality testing?

We inspect visible conditions and airflow. We do not operate an accredited laboratory or provide medical advice. If a formal air quality test is needed, we can explain what to ask a qualified testing provider.

Will duct cleaning help my allergies?

It may reduce one source of dust and irritants, but it is not a medical treatment. Pets, pollen, humidity, and filtration also matter.

Do you repair furnaces?

No. We clean accessible furnace components as part of duct cleaning, but we do not repair or service furnaces.

What if the crawl space is damp?

That is a common source of musty odors. We inspect the vapor barrier, insulation, and visible moisture, then recommend cleanup, repair, or encapsulation as appropriate.
