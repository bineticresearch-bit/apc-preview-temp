Why Portland homeowners call All Pro

Portland's housing stock is a mix of early-1900s bungalows, mid-century ranches, and newer condos and infill. Each type has its own duct and moisture patterns. Older homes often have original ductwork, finished basements, and crawl spaces that have seen decades of remodels.

All Pro serves Portland neighborhoods on both sides of the river. We clean supply and return ducts, dryer vents, and crawl spaces, and we explain the condition we find before recommending work.

Local conditions

What we commonly see in Portland

  • Pre-1940 bungalows often have small or modified duct runs and decades of accumulated dust.
  • Basement remodels and additions can leave construction debris in the duct system.
  • Portland's wet season keeps crawl spaces damp, especially where vapor barriers are torn or missing.
  • Condos and tight closets can hide long, flexible dryer vent runs that are hard to inspect.
