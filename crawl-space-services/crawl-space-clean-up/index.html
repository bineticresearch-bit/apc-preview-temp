Crawl Space Cleanup in Vancouver, WA & Portland, OR

Debris, rodent mess, and damp insulation create odors that can drift into living space.

The crawl space is out of sight, but it is not separate from the home. Air and moisture move between the crawl space and the living area. Debris, rodent droppings, wet insulation, and mold can contribute to musty odors and a less healthy indoor environment.

Cleanup starts with removing the material that is causing the problem, not masking it with a fragrance. We remove loose debris, vacuum surfaces with HEPA equipment where appropriate, sanitize affected areas, and identify the moisture or entry problem that allowed the mess to develop.

What is included

  • A crawl space inspection with photos of visible conditions.
  • Removal of loose debris, fallen insulation, and rodent nesting material.
  • HEPA vacuuming of accessible surfaces.
  • Sanitizing of affected areas after debris removal.
  • Identification of moisture, entry points, and damaged vapor barrier sections.
  • A written summary of what was removed and what still needs repair.

Signs you may need this service

  • A musty odor drifts into the living space.
  • You hear scratching or find droppings in the crawl space.
  • Insulation is hanging down, wet, or torn apart.
  • The vapor barrier is missing, torn, or covered in debris.
  • The crawl space feels damp or has standing water.

How the service works

Inspect and document

We check access, visible moisture, insulation, vapor barrier, and signs of pests.

Remove the debris

Loose material and damaged insulation are bagged and removed. Accessible surfaces are HEPA vacuumed.

Sanitize

Affected areas are cleaned and sanitized. We avoid covering up an active moisture problem.

Report and repair plan

We explain what caused the mess and what repair, exclusion, or barrier work is needed next.

Frequently asked questions

Do you remove rodents?

We clean up the mess rodents leave behind. Trapping and removal of live animals may need a licensed pest professional, depending on the situation. We will tell you what we find.

Is crawl space cleanup safe?

We use protective equipment and controlled methods. If there is extensive contamination, standing water, or structural damage, we explain the limits before work begins.

Will cleanup get rid of the smell?

Removing the source usually improves odors. If moisture or a damaged vapor barrier remains, the smell can return until that cause is corrected.

Do you repair the vapor barrier too?

Yes. Vapor barrier repair or replacement can be included in the scope, and we will recommend it if the barrier is damaged.
