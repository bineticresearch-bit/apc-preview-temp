Crawl space services

One company for the whole system

A crawl space is part of the home's air and moisture envelope. When it is damp, dirty, or open to pests, the effects show up upstairs. Our crawl space services address the mess, the moisture, and the entry points that create the problem.

We inspect the space, explain what we find, and recommend a scope that fits the condition of the home. Cleanup, exclusion, insulation, mold remediation, and vapor barrier work can be combined when that is the most practical approach.

Good questions

Common questions about crawl space service

Why does crawl space moisture matter?

Moisture in the crawl space can damage insulation, framing, and ductwork, and it can contribute to musty odors in the living space. A functioning vapor barrier, drainage, and ventilation are all part of the solution.

Can you clean up rodent mess?

Yes. We remove nesting material, droppings, and damaged insulation, then HEPA vacuum and sanitize affected areas. Live animal removal may require a licensed pest professional; we will explain what we find.

Do you repair vapor barriers?

Yes. We repair or replace damaged vapor barriers, including fitting the material around pipes and supports and sealing the seams and edges.

Do you treat mold?

We remove affected material, HEPA vacuum accessible surfaces, clean and treat affected framing, and identify the moisture source. Lasting remediation requires correcting the moisture, not just treating the visible growth.
