Coupon Air Duct Cleaning: What the $49 Special Really Costs

A very low advertised price is usually not the final price. Here is how to read the fine print and compare duct cleaning quotes fairly.

September 15, 2026Consumer adviceBy All Pro Duct Cleaning

A $49 or $99 air duct cleaning coupon is one of the most common advertisements in the trades. It gets attention because a whole-home cleaning sounds like a bargain. In practice, the advertised price often covers a limited service, and the final invoice can be much higher.

How the low-price offer works

The offer may include one vent, one furnace, one main trunk, or a visual inspection rather than a full cleaning. Once the crew is inside, they may explain that the price does not cover the rest of the system and present additional charges. By then, the homeowner has already set aside the time and may feel pressured to proceed.

Not every company that advertises a special is dishonest. Some use a low entry price for a genuinely limited service and explain the scope clearly. The problem is the offers that do not disclose what is included until the technician is at the door.

Questions to ask before booking

  • How many vents does the advertised price include?
  • Does it cover both supply and return ducts?
  • Is the furnace, blower area, or plenum included?
  • Is the price guaranteed, or can it change after an inspection?
  • What equipment is used, and how is the system put under negative pressure?
  • Is the dryer vent included?
  • Are there trip charges, disposal fees, or other add-ons?

Get the answers in writing or in a text message before the appointment. A legitimate company should be able to explain the scope without hedging.

Red flags to watch for

  • The advertised price is dramatically lower than other local quotes.
  • The company will not quote the full job by phone.
  • The quote changes significantly once the technician arrives.
  • The crew cannot explain how the system is put under negative pressure.
  • They use a small shop vacuum rather than a high-volume HEPA unit.
  • They pressure you to add sanitization, encapsulation, or repairs on the spot.
  • They will not show you the material that came out of the system.

What a fair quote looks like

A fair duct cleaning quote is based on the size of the home and the number of vents. It should state what is included, how long the work will take, and whether the dryer vent and accessible furnace components are part of the service. The price should be the price when the work is finished.
